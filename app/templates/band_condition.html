Algorithm

Short explanation of what drives the estimate.

Reference stations estimate the local baseline.

Configured fixed stations show whether local audibility is below, near, or above its long-term norm for this hour.

Traffic estimates occupancy, not opening by itself.

Total and mobile traffic are compared against their hour-of-day baseline to detect likely channel load or local congestion.

Rare fixed stations indicate opening.

If fixed stations that are usually absent begin to appear, they raise the opening score and can outweigh pure occupancy effects.
